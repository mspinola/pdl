          Scoring by era

          Average points per team per game

          Scoring drifts up from 66 a game in 2000 to 78 by 2017, then jumps to 116 in 2018 and stays there, which is a scoring change rather than better teams. This is why every points figure on this page is indexed to its own era. The jump lands in the same season the one franchise changed hands, so raw points would have flattered the new owner for no footballing reason.

          Two sources. 2008 to 2026 is the MyFantasyLeague export log: machine-written, dated to the day, assets named by player and pick. 2000 to 2007 is the league's own archive at mspinola.github.io, hand-written in prose, where the two teams are reliable but the assets are only a sentence. MyFantasyLeague keeps no transaction log for 2003 to 2006 and one record for 2007, which is why the archive carries those years.

          Read the early seasons as a floor. The archive was kept by hand, so a trade missing from it is invisible here. Its offseason sections are also labelled a year early: its "2007 offseason" holds exactly the deals MyFantasyLeague logs in 2008, which is how the offset was established and corrected.

          Asset counts are a floor too. 24 of the MyFantasyLeague records name only one side's assets. Those trades and the two teams on them are right, but what changed hands is understated. Three deals were written as two records each and are merged.

          Games. 3,046 games, 2000 to 2026. The 2003 to 2026 results come from MyFantasyLeague's weekly schedule and the 2000 to 2002 results from the archive's schedule pages. The league's own lastRegularSeasonWeek setting is not maintained, reading 16 in seasons that plainly had three playoff rounds, so the regular season is separated instead by the playoff brackets MFL publishes and, before those exist, by the weeks that carry fewer than a full slate of games. MFL also writes an unplayed fixture as a tie with no score, which would have added 56 phantom ties to the current season, so a game counts only with a real score.

          Identity. Teams are linked across seasons by name plus dynasty roster overlap, which reproduces the known mapping exactly over 2016 to 2026 where MyFantasyLeague ids are stable. The one owner change in league history is applied by hand, and the 2001 expansion is taken from the 2000 standings and the 2001 expansion rules.
