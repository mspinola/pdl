### 6.2 Playoff Tie-Breaking Scheme

- **2014**: Ties for a playoff place are broken by: 1. head-to-head result (skipped when three or more teams are tied), 2. points, 3. a coin toss.
  - Sources: changes.html, 2014 meeting
- **2023**: For a tie of three or more teams, the top team is seeded first, then the tiebreak restarts among the rest for the next seed.
  - Sources: confirmed by the commissioner, 2026-10-29, league poll 2023 (Break the tie all at once (big bang) 4, Advance the top team, then restart for the next seed (rinse and repeat) 5, Don't care 4) and email 2023-08-17, "PDL"
- **2025** *(in practice)*: In practice a two-team tie goes to the head-to-head winner, and a split head-to-head goes to points.
  - Sources: message board 2025-12-09

### 7.2 Free Agents / Waivers

- **2008**: Claims are processed once a week by the league website (Wednesday night). Waiver order is season-long and rolling: a team whose claim succeeds drops to the bottom. After the run, every player not just dropped is a free agent, first come first served, until his game kicks off.
  - Sources: changes.html, 2008 meeting and message board 2008-08-25
- **2025**: A player dropped in the waiver run is a free agent, first come first served, straight after it, instead of waiting a week. A player dropped between the run and his kickoff is available at once, not put on waivers.
  - Sources: message board 2025-09-01, message board 2025-09-01, league poll 2025 (Keep as is 5, Make them immediate free agents, first come first served 6) and league poll 2025 (Keep as is 6, Available first come first served immediately after waivers 7)

### 8.1 Offensive Points

- **2010**: Yardage scores 1 point per 10 yards once a threshold is reached: 200 yards passing for a QB, 50 yards rushing or receiving otherwise. This replaces the yardage bonuses. TEs unchanged.
  - Evidence: MFL's scoring rules: QB passing changes from 5 points at 200 and 1 per 10 from 250 (2003 to 2009) to 1 per 10 from 200 (2010 to 2017). WR receiving loses its 100-yard tier. RB rushing keeps a 5-point step at 50 yards, then 1 per 10 from 60, which no written source mentions.
  - Sources: changes.html, 2010 meeting
- **2018**: Yardage scores from the first yard, with no thresholds: 1 point per 10 yards rushing or receiving, 1 per 20 yards passing.
  - Sources: email 2018-07-26, "Summer Meeting Updates"
- **2018**: Yardage scores in fractions: 0.1 point per yard rushing or receiving, 0.1 per 2 yards passing.
  - Evidence: MFL's scoring rules from 2018: 0.1 per yard rushing and receiving, 0.1 per 2 yards passing, all from zero.
  - Sources: email 2018-07-26, "Summer Meeting Updates"
- **2018**: 0.5 points per reception, for every position. Passed 10 to 6.
  - Evidence: MFL's scoring rules from 2018: receptions at 0.5 for QB, RB, WR and TE.
  - Sources: email 2018-08-01, "Re: Summer Meeting Updates (voting update)" and league poll 2018 (Yes 10, No 5)
