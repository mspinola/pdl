# The ones that got away

Generated by `python3 src/got_away.py`. Every in-season drop from 2008, and what the player did next for other teams: the rest of that season and all of the next. Value is points above the average starter at the player's position that season, counted in that season's average starts, so positions and eras compare: 5 means five average starts' worth of points beyond a typical starter. Kickers and defenses are shown, though they are dropped and re-added as a matter of routine.

3,690 in-season drops. 1,760 of the dropped players started for another team within the horizon, and 43 were worth 5 or more average starts above a typical starter at their position.

## Notes

- A player can be dropped more than once in a season, by different teams. Each drop is its own row.
