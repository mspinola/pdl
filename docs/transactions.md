## Does working the wire help?

Across the 17 owners, adds per season since 2008 against career win rate: r = -0.02. Against points scored (indexed to the era): r = -0.01. With this few owners, a correlation under about 0.5 in either direction is indistinguishable from none, and the arrow could run either way: a struggling team has more holes to fill.

## Notes on the data

- 2000 to 2007 come from the league's own transaction pages, 2008 on from MyFantasyLeague's log.
- MFL logged first-come pickups as waiver moves until 2016. They are separated by time: a claim carries the timestamp of the weekly waiver run. From 2017, when MFL logs the two apart, every claim sits at a run.
- Weeks run Tuesday to Monday, so a Tuesday or Wednesday add counts toward the coming Thursday's week.
- Couchfire's figures start in 2018 and Bunchalosers' end in 2017, the one change of owner.
- Trades are in the trades analysis, not here. IR moves (2020 and 2026) are not counted as adds.
