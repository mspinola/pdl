# Swami Says, week 9

Year to date record: 0 - 0.

### Carter's Commandos at Couchfire

**Pick: Couchfire.** The Commandos are 5-3 and the Swami still can't figure out how. They put up 58.9 last week with three starters on zero, and Doreen filed a report. Couchfire is 3-5 but just hung 164.3 on the Dogs, and Joe Burrow scored 69.1 by himself, which is more than the Commandos' whole lineup managed. Burrow, Gibbs and Chase, Couchfire in a laugher.

### OakalDoakals at Texas Nashvillians

**Pick: Texas Nashvillians.** Eight and oh, first in scoring by a mile, Josh Allen on 311 for the year. The Doakals have lost six straight and started Emanuel Wilson on a zero last week. The Swami doesn't do mercy rules. Nashvillians roll to 9-0 and the Doakals move one step closer to the first pick.

### Tally Wackers at Scooter's Boozers

**Pick: Scooter's Boozers.** The Wackers are a slot short with Freiermuth and Wan'Dale Robinson on the Pittsburgh bye and Jonathon Brooks on IR, and that's after starting Stroud, Brooks and Brian Robinson on zeros last week. The Boozers lose Warren, Rodgers and Metcalf to the same bye, but Jordan Love's 235.9 doesn't take weeks off. Boozers make it five in a row.

### Dunbar Dogs at Beers

**Pick: Beers.** On paper the Dogs score more, 104.9 a week to the Beers' 95.6, but paper doesn't start Cam Ward, Chris Boswell and the Titans defense on a bye. Two empty slots, and the Swami counted twice. Mahomes dropped 43.6 on the Wackers last week. Beers win it, and the Dogs go to 2-7 wondering where the lineup went.

### Da Funk at Otters

**Pick: Otters.** Game of the week. Da Funk is 8-0 with Drake Maye, and the Swami respects that, right up until he looks at the history: the Otters own this series 11-3 and have beaten Da Funk all three times in the playoffs. Kirk Cousins put up 45.6 last week in a loss, which tells you about the rest of the lineup, but the Otters are second in the league in scoring for a reason. MFL has Zay Flowers projected for 74.7 and the Swami doesn't believe that either. Otters hand Da Funk its first loss, in a shootout.

### Blitzkrieg at Iron City Pounders

**Pick: Blitzkrieg.** The champs are 4-4 and fourth in scoring, which is what a 1-3 division record will do to you. Trevor Lawrence and Trey McBride carry them. The Pounders left 52.3 on the bench last week, lost by 31.1, and now Tony Pollard is on the Tennessee bye. Blitzkrieg wins going away.

### Manic Moose at Ho Train

**Pick: Manic Moose.** The Ho Train leads the all-time series 9-4, and that's the last nice thing the Swami has to say. Dead last in scoring, 740 points, Jaxson Dart on IR. The Moose started Lamar Jackson on a zero last week and still won by 36, so with Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Stafford doing the work they don't need him. Moose, and it isn't close.

### F-Jackie at Karpet Munchers

**Pick: Karpet Munchers.** Upset special. MFL likes F-Jackie by 2.9, and F-Jackie has won two straight on the strength of Braelon Allen and the Jets ground game, with Michael Penix Jr. scoring zero last week. The Munchers lead this series 14-5, have beaten F-Jackie both times in the playoffs, and have Goff, Nacua and Bryce Young. Swami says take the Munchers and the history.
