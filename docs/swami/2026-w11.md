# Swami Says, week 11

Year to date record: 10 - 6.

### Carter's Commandos at Blitzkrieg

**Pick: Blitzkrieg.** The Commandos still lead the AFC East at 5-5, which says more about the AFC East than it does about the Commandos. They have lost three straight, and now Bijan Robinson, Drew Lock and Sam Darnold all sit on the bye. The champs have won three in a row, hung 195.1 on the Dogs last week, and Christian McCaffrey scored 83.3 of it by himself. The last time these two met in the playoffs it was 190.6 to 188.2. This one is not that. Blitzkrieg in a laugher.

### OakalDoakals at Da Funk

**Pick: Da Funk.** The Swami picked the Doakals last week and the Doakals paid him back, so he says this with love: no. Da Funk is 10-0 and has Drake Maye, A.J. Brown, Quinshon Judkins and Christian Watson all on the bye, and MFL still has them by 48.2. The Doakals lose Cooper Kupp and the Rams defense to the bye, Jordan Addison is out, and they are a slot short. Amon-Ra St. Brown and Kenneth Walker III are plenty. Da Funk to 11-0, and with a win the AFC West is theirs.

### Tally Wackers at Manic Moose

**Pick: Manic Moose.** Upset special. MFL has the Wackers by 1.6, and the Swami would like to know what MFL has been watching. The Wackers have lost four straight, they are a slot short with Wan'Dale Robinson on IR, and they started Jacory Croskey-Merritt on a zero last week. Yes, the Moose lose Stafford, Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Tucker Kraft to the bye. Geno Smith has 210.5 on the year and Bhayshul Tuten has 171.8, and the Moose are 5-3 against the Wackers in the playoffs. At 6-4 the Moose cannot afford to give this one away, and they won't. Moose in a squeaker.

### Dunbar Dogs at F-Jackie

**Pick: Dunbar Dogs.** The Dogs scored 173 last week and lost, which is the most Dogs thing the Swami has ever seen. Emmett Johnson put up 50.1 of it. F-Jackie scored 67.5 against the Doakals, sits 13th in scoring, and has Michael Penix Jr. on the bye. The Dogs are a slot short with Marvin Harrison Jr. and Woody Marks out, and F-Jackie won this game 160.3 to 54.4 a year ago. The Swami doesn't think so either. Jayden Daniels and Bucky Irving are enough, Dogs in a low-scoring win.

### Scooter's Boozers at Karpet Munchers

**Pick: Scooter's Boozers.** The Munchers lead the NFC West at 5-5, lead this series 21-7-1, and scored 66.6 last week. The Swami will let you guess which of those three numbers matters. The Boozers have won six straight and George Pickens dropped 44 on the Otters. Jordan Love is on the bye, but Aaron Rodgers has 203.1 on the year, and the Munchers are 14th in scoring and a slot short. Boozers make it seven, comfortably.

### Beers at Ho Train

**Pick: Beers.** Upset special. MFL likes the Ho Train by 14.6, and the Swami liked the Ho Train last week too, right up until they lost by 114.9. Never again. The Beers are two slots short with Jason Myers and Demario Douglas on the bye, and the Swami does not care, because Patrick Mahomes scored 63.9 last week and the entire Ho Train lineup scored 56.7. The Ho Train is 16th of 16 in scoring, loses Denzel Boston and Zach Charbonnet to the bye, and Brock Bowers is questionable. The series is even at 12-12. Beers take the lead in an ugly one.

### Couchfire at Otters

**Pick: Couchfire.** The Swami called the Otters an upset special two weeks running and the Otters hosed him both times. He is a slow learner, not a non-learner. The Otters have lost three straight and now Kyren Williams and Blake Corum sit on the Rams bye. Couchfire has won three in a row, scored 171.6 last week, and Joe Burrow leads them with 373.2 on the year. Both teams need it, with Couchfire at 5-5 and the Otters at 4-6, a game back in the NFC West. Burrow, Gibbs and Chase win it by more than the 3.9 MFL is laying.

### Texas Nashvillians at Iron City Pounders

**Pick: Texas Nashvillians.** The Swami's lock of the week, again. The Pounders lead this series 12-9 and beat the Nashvillians 127.6 to 104.2 a year ago, and the Swami is sure that is a lovely memory. They have lost four straight and left 54.2 on the bench last week. The Nashvillians are 10-0, first in scoring at 141.8 a week, and won their last game by 100. They lose Drake London and Matthew Golden to the bye and it will not matter, because Josh Allen has 373.6 on the year. Nashvillians to 11-0 in a laugher.
