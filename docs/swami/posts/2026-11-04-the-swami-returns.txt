You remember the Swami. All of you were here for him except Couchfire, the Beers and the Doakals, and the rest of you have spent 26 years trying to forget. Allow him to reintroduce himself.

Back in 1999 and 2000 the Swami picked every game, every week, on the old site, with a reason for each one and a record that he published himself, which is more than any of you have ever done. He went 46-35 in the FFL in 2000. He does not want to talk about 1999.

The PDL had a Swami that year too. A guest. He wrote in all capital letters, he could not spell, he hated the Tally Wackers for reasons he never explained, and he used words that the Office of the Commissioner has since asked us not to repeat. He finished 49-42 and was never heard from again. The real Swami wishes him well, wherever he is, and assumes it is a bar.

So here is the deal for 2026. Every Thursday morning, before the first kickoff, the Swami picks all eight games with a reason tied to a player, a bye, a streak or a piece of history the Swami remembers and you would rather he didn't. Every Tuesday the office marks them right or wrong and the record goes up for everyone to see. Week 9 is already up:
