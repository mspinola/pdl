# Swami Says, week 12

Year to date record: 15 - 9.

### Carter's Commandos at Tally Wackers

**Pick: Carter's Commandos.** Upset special. The Commandos have lost four straight and still lead the AFC East at 5-6, so the Swami has stopped asking questions about the AFC East. MFL has the Wackers by 15.5, and the Wackers earned it by beating the Moose last week, which cost the Swami an upset special and he has not forgotten. But the Commandos are 4-0 in the division, won this game 98.2 to 94.6 in week 5, and get Bijan Robinson back from the bye next to Javonte Williams, who scored 34.6 last week. C.J. Stroud can't do it alone twice in a row. Commandos in a squeaker.

### OakalDoakals at Dunbar Dogs

**Pick: Dunbar Dogs.** The Doakals beat Da Funk by 59.2 last week, with Dak Prescott scoring 59.1 and Ashton Jeanty 43.4, and the Swami was on the wrong end of every point. He is not chasing it. The Doakals are 0-4 in the AFC East, Jordan Addison is doubtful, and the Dogs won the week 5 meeting 153.4 to 78.9. Jayden Daniels, Bucky Irving and Emmett Johnson keep the Dogs alive in a division where 5-7 might be first place. Dogs by double digits.

### Da Funk at Manic Moose

**Pick: Da Funk.** A Da Funk win clinches the AFC West. The Swami typed that same sentence last week and Da Funk scored 94.7, so this time he means it. Drake Maye is out, and it does not matter, because Fernando Mendoza put up 39.5 in his place and Amon-Ra St. Brown, Christian Watson and George Kittle are all in the lineup. The Moose lost to the Wackers last week, lost this game 163.4 to 89.9 in week 5, and Lamar Jackson is on IR. The Moose lead the series 37-15, and none of those 37 help this week. Da Funk in a romp.

### Blitzkrieg at F-Jackie

**Pick: Blitzkrieg.** The champs have won four straight and left 33.5 on the bench last week just to keep it interesting. F-Jackie has lost three straight, sits 13th in scoring, and projects to 68.8 against Trevor Lawrence, Christian McCaffrey and Trey McBride. F-Jackie leads this series 25-24. Make it 25-25. Blitzkrieg in a laugher.

### Scooter's Boozers at Couchfire

**Pick: Scooter's Boozers.** Upset special, and the game of the week. The Boozers have won seven straight, Couchfire has won four straight, and MFL likes Couchfire by 3.6 because Joe Burrow has 403.2 on the year. The Swami likes Burrow too. He likes this better: Couchfire is 0-4 in the NFC East, lost the week 5 meeting 159.5 to 138.8, and lost both games to the Boozers a year ago. George Pickens, Jameson Williams and Jordan Love make it eight, and the Boozers stay on the Nashvillians' heels. Boozers in a squeaker.

### Beers at Texas Nashvillians

**Pick: Texas Nashvillians.** The Swami called the Nashvillians his lock of the week and they lost to a 2-8 team by 14. He would like it noted that he is doing it again. The Beers have won two straight behind Patrick Mahomes, who scored 40.2 last week, but they are 15th in scoring and lost this game 160 to 59.4 in week 5. Josh Allen has 388.8 on the year and MFL projects him for 32.3 by himself. The Beers lead the all time series 32-14, and it will be 32-15 after this one. Nashvillians in a laugher, and they had better.

### Ho Train at Otters

**Pick: Otters.** The Swami has picked the Otters to win twice this year and they hosed him both times. He has picked the Ho Train once and they lost by 114.9. So this is a choice between two teams that owe him money. The Ho Train is 16th of 16 in scoring at 89.4 a week, Brock Bowers and Denzel Boston are both questionable, and the Otters won the week 5 meeting 144.4 to 110.1. The Otters have lost four straight and are still fifth in the league in scoring, and at 3-1 in the NFC West they are one game out of first. Kirk Cousins and Parker Washington end the skid. Otters, comfortably.

### Karpet Munchers at Iron City Pounders

**Pick: Iron City Pounders.** The Pounders ruined the Swami's lock last week, 124.6 to 110.6 over the Nashvillians, and the Swami respects a team that can ruin a lock. Justin Herbert scored 42.7 of it and is questionable, and the Pounders still have Deshaun Watson, Jonathan Taylor and Derrick Henry. The Munchers lead the NFC West at 5-6, have lost two straight, started Courtland Sutton on a zero and left 31.1 on the bench. They won this one 135.6 to 88.1 in week 5, and that was a long time ago. Pounders in a low-scoring win.

## Playoff picture

- Three weeks left. Da Funk and the Texas Nashvillians are both 10-1, and both lost last week with the Swami's name on the ticket.
- AFC West: Da Funk is three games up on Blitzkrieg at 7-4. A Da Funk win or a Blitzkrieg loss this week clinches the division.
- AFC East: the Commandos lead at 5-6 on a four-game losing streak, with the Dogs and Wackers a game back at 4-7. Somebody may win this division with a losing record and the Swami can't wait.
- NFC East: the Boozers are 9-2 with seven straight wins and still a game back of the Nashvillians. Couchfire at 6-5 is playing for a wild card.
- NFC West: the Munchers lead at 5-6, with the Otters and Ho Train a game back at 4-7. If the Swami has this week right, the Munchers and the Otters are tied at 5-7 when the week is over.
