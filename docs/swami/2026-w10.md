### Scooter's Boozers at Otters

### Beers at Iron City Pounders

**Pick: Iron City Pounders.** Two teams going nowhere, so somebody has to win. The Beers are 15th in scoring and a slot short with Josh Jacobs out and J.K. Dobbins on the bye. The Pounders lose Waddle, Goedert and Makai Lemon to byes, but Tyler Shough, Derrick Henry and Jonathan Taylor are enough. Pounders snap a three-game skid in a squeaker.

### Couchfire at Ho Train

**Pick: Ho Train.** Upset special. The Ho Train hosed the Swami last week, 90.9 to 63.8 over the Moose, and the Swami knows when to stop fighting. MFL likes Couchfire by 3.9, but the Ho Train leads this series 6-2 and won the 2025 meeting 117.6 to 100.9. Couchfire is a slot short with Cairo Santos on the bye, and Joe Burrow can't kick field goals. Brock Bowers and Jacoby Brissett pull it out, and the Ho Train climbs to 5-5 in the West.

### Texas Nashvillians at Karpet Munchers

**Pick: Texas Nashvillians.** The Swami's lock of the week. Puka Nacua is out, Courtland Sutton and Kenneth Gainwell are on the bye, and the Munchers project to 65.7 against a team averaging 139.1. Josh Allen has 330.3 on the year and MFL projects him for 35 by himself. The Munchers lead the NFC West at 5-4 and are about to find out what 5-5 feels like. Nashvillians to 10-0 in a laugher.
