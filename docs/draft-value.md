# Draft value

Generated by `python3 src/draft_value.py`. Every pick from 2008, judged over 3 seasons (the draft season and the two after) for the team that made it. Starts are regular-season starts. Value is points above the average starter at the player's position, counted in that season's average starts. A hit is 10 or more starts. Owner figures use drafts with all 3 seasons played (2008 to 2023).

## Notes

- From 2008, where MFL kept the draft and the lineups.
- Starts count only for the team that drafted the player. A pick traded away, or a player traded after the draft, stops counting for the drafter, which is the point: value to the team that spent the pick.
- A dynasty pick can pay later than three seasons. The horizon is a choice, and the CSV has every pick for a longer view.
