# Draft value

Generated by `python3 src/draft_value.py`. Every pick from 2008, judged over 3 seasons: the draft season and the two after.

A pick is a **hit** if the player made 10 or more regular-season starts in those seasons for any team. In a dynasty league a drafted player is also currency: the Moose took DK Metcalf 7th overall in 2019, started him twice, and traded him that October with two others for three players, after which he started 33 times. That pick worked, and a measure that counts only the drafting team's starts would call it a bust. So the drafting team's starts are a second measure, **kept**: 10 or more starts for the team that made the pick. Value is points above the average starter at the player's position that season, counted in that season's average starts. Owner figures use drafts with all 3 seasons played (2008 to 2023).

## Owners

Over slot is the average number of starts a pick gave above what picks at that point in the draft usually give, so an owner is not rewarded for drafting early. The first is for any team (did he pick players who became starters), the second for his own team (did he keep them). The margin is two standard errors on the first: inside it, a figure is indistinguishable from an average drafter. With 17 owners, about one lands outside it by chance alone. Started elsewhere is the share of his draftees' starts made for other teams. Kickers and defenses are left out, here and in every list below.
