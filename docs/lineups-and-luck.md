## Schedule luck (2000 on)

Expected wins are what a team's weekly scores were worth against everyone that week, not just the opponent it drew. Luck is actual wins minus expected wins. Over a 13 or 14 game season, plus or minus one win is ordinary, and two or more is a lot.

Across 414 completed team-seasons, all-play rate and actual win rate correlate at r = 0.79: scoring decides most of the standings, and the schedule the rest.
